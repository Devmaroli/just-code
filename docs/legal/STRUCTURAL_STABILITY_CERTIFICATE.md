## 2. Design wind (worked example for a 12 m hub height, Terrain Category 2)

Vz = Vb × k1 × k2 × k3 × k4  
Vz = 39 × 1.06 × 1.00 × 1.00 × 1.00 = **41.34 m/s**  
Design pressure pz = 0.6 × Vz² = 0.6 × 1,708.9956 = **1.025 kN/m²**  
Force-coefficient Cf for a sharp-edged rectangular billboard: **1.20** (IS 875 Part 3, Table 23 / equivalent).  
Area per face (including 50 mm cabinet frame): 8.10 m × 4.10 m = 33.21 m².  
Along-wind force per face Fw = pz × Cf × A = 1.025 × 1.20 × 33.21 = **40.82 kN**.  
Dual-face: design for **vector combination** of both faces with a 15° yaw case and a 0° case; torsion from unequal dimming/soiling included as 10% eccentricity.

The undersigned certifies that member stresses and foundation bearing / uplift remain within allowable limits for the above loading, including a monsoon residual-life case with 20% section-loss on unprotected welds (the specification nonetheless requires a full coastal paint system so that this case is a robustness check, not the intended corrosion state).
